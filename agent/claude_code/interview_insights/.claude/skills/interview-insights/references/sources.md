# Source guide

Where to search, and which report section each kind of source belongs to. A source belongs to the section
of its kind, however convincing its content is.

## Section A: candidates and company insiders

First-hand accounts by people who took the interview or work at the company and interview candidates.

- Interview reviews: Glassdoor (Interviews), Indeed (Interviews), Blind (TeamBlind), Levels.fyi.
- Candidate threads: LeetCode Discuss (Interview Experience, company tags), Reddit (`r/cscareerquestions`,
  `r/ExperiencedDevs`, `r/leetcode`, the company's subreddit), Hacker News comments.
- Personal write-ups: blog posts, Medium or dev.to articles, and LinkedIn posts in which the author
  describes their own interview at the company, or describes interviewing candidates as an employee.
- Talks and podcasts in which an employee describes how the company interviews.

The account must be first-hand. A post retelling someone else's experience goes to section C.

## Section B: the company itself

- The company's careers site: interview process pages, "how we hire", preparation guides, FAQ.
- The job posting for the position (requirements and stated interview steps).
- The company's engineering blog and its official social media accounts.
- Official material the company gives candidates, when it is published.

## Section C: less trusted sources and own assumptions

- Aggregators and question banks that do not show who reported a question or when: interview question
  lists, "top N questions at <company>" articles, prep course marketing pages.
- Second-hand retellings, anonymous claims without detail, and AI-generated content.
- Information about similar companies or the same company's other roles, used as an analogy.
- The agent's own knowledge, always marked as an assumption and never given a source or date.

## Dates

- Use the date of the account itself (review date, post date), not the date a page was last updated.
- A Glassdoor or Blind entry shows its date; a blog post usually does in its header or URL.
- When the date cannot be established, the source goes to section C as `undated`.
